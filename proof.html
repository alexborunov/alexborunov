> proof · what others wrote, measured and awarded

Built from nothing. Checked by people who owe me nothing.

I started at a cancer charity in St Petersburg with no budget and a team you could count on one hand. What I built there ended up in an oncology journal, at ASCO, in the national business press, in Wikipedia and on an awards stage. None of it is written by me. That is the point of this page.

Nominated by the U.S. Embassy and selected for a fully funded State Department exchange for nonprofit leaders from Russia. Meetings with American nonprofits and foundations, including cancer support organisations, in Washington, D.C., San Francisco, Houston and New York.
