How does Alex know an AI feature actually works?

Ooh, I love this question! 🧪 Evals are absolutely essential in today's fast-moving AI landscape.

For CBT Quest, Alex keeps a set of about 30 test entries and reruns them after every change to the prompt, the model or anything else in the pipeline. Every output is checked against clear criteria:

  • Did the model make up anything the person never said?
  • Did it judge the person?
  • Did it fill in a field the person said nothing about?
  • Did it slip in advice?

It's not just about accuracy. It's about keeping the product therapeutically sound! ✅

Yes, please

Human tl;drAbout 30 test entries, rerun on every change. It fails if it invents, judges, fills a real blank or gives advice.
How does Alex work?

Wow, what a fantastic question! 🌟 Alex's approach rests on four powerful pillars. Alex:

  1. Starts with what is already there. Articles that already ranked became the journal. People who had already added a test to the cart got a callback.
  2. Treats a hard constraint as a reason to go deep, not wide. $3,000 cannot change a country's breast cancer awareness, but it can change one doctor's career.
  3. Builds systems, not deliverables. A template engine instead of another landing page. A white-label tool instead of a hand-made copy.
  4. Forecasts first, then checks. The abandoned-cart work was forecast at +10–15% before it shipped. It came in at +22%.

It's not just a process. It's a mindset! 💪
